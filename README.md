# AI Prompts for Building with Intent

Prompts I created while building digital products at scale. These aren't theories—they're what happened when I stopped asking vague questions and started being specific about what I *didn't* want to change.

**Context:** Techno-anthropologist + AI strategist + 20+ years building enterprise systems. These prompts emerged from real work on migration tech, trade compliance platforms, and digital exports.

## Core Prompts

### 1. **CSS Fix Everything** 🎨
**Problem:** You define a brand system. Nobody uses it. Colors exist but stay invisible. Buttons feel random.

**Solution:** One instruction applies your entire palette at once. No HTML changes. Instant coherence.

→ **[Read the full prompt](./prompts/01-css-fix-everything.md)**

**When to use:**
- Redesigning without reorganizing
- Activating an unused brand palette
- Fixing button hierarchy in existing code
- Adding accessibility focus states without touching HTML

**Output:** ~600 lines of CSS, fully commented, zero redundancy. Colors from your palette finally *visible*.

---

### 2. **Three-Lens Site Audit** 🔍
**Problem:** You built a website. Three different people will see three different problems. You're only fixing one.

**Solution:** Evaluate through three lenses—editorial, user trust, and engineering—then separate narrative problems from technical debt.

→ **[Read the full prompt](./prompts/02-three-lens-audit.md)**

**When to use:**
- Before redesigning anything
- When a site "feels off" but you can't say why
- To align stakeholders on what to fix first
- To uncover hidden UX problems before launch

**Output:** Coherence score (0-10), 15+ actionable findings, prioritized by impact.

---

### 3. **Product Page Architecture** 📋
**Problem:** You design each product page from scratch. No two look the same. You fight for coherence forever.

**Solution:** See the skeleton. Hero → Headline → Layer → Product → Copy → Accent → Button. Fill it once. Stamp it forever.

→ **[Read the full prompt](./prompts/03-product-page-template.md)**

**When to use:**
- Building a portfolio with 5+ products
- Launching a collection that needs visual unity
- Scaling a design system without a designer
- Creating content that speaks with one voice

**Output:** One structure. Fifty pages. Coherence automatic.

---

## How These Work Together

1. **Start with the Three-Lens Audit** — know what your site looks like to three different people
2. **Apply CSS Fix Everything** — activate what you defined but never used
3. **Use Product Page Architecture** — scale consistency without reinventing

## Who This Is For

- **Founders** building without a design team
- **Solo developers** who know code but not design
- **Product people** frustrated with "it just feels off"
- **Migrant professionals** building across languages and borders
- **Anyone** who thinks better with structure than blank canvas

## Important Notes

These prompts assume:
- You use Claude (or compatible AI)
- You want to be specific instead of vague
- You value clarity over novelty
- You're building something that matters

They don't require:
- A design background
- An external design tool
- A large team
- Perfect English (I'm bilingual, not precious about grammar)

## The Philosophy

Sharing is caring. If we the AI people don't share the knowledge, AI will keep being something people fear.

These prompts exist because I rebuilt my website three times and learned something obvious each time. I'm sharing so you don't have to rebuild three times.
