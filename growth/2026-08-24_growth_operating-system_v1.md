## Content validation framework (gate before anything ships)

A piece must accomplish at least one: recruit testers · start conversations · gather feedback · build trust · earn shares · earn saves · attract collaborators · attract customers. If it does none — rewrite or kill. A beautiful post that does nothing is inventory.

## Current-evidence growth principles (updated 2026-08; re-verify quarterly)

- **Small IG accounts (<10k):** Reels-first for reach; shares and saves are the top ranking signals; engagement *velocity* (first 30 min) outweighs totals — publish when she can stay 30 min to answer every comment with real sentences (conversation threads are a high-weight signal). 3–5 specific hashtags as categorization, not 30 generic ones. Quality consistency beats daily filler — 3 good beats 7 mediocre. ([later.com](https://later.com/blog/how-instagram-algorithm-works/), [sproutsocial.com](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/instagram-algorithm/), [mirra.my](https://www.mirra.my/en/blog/instagram-algorithm-2026-complete-analysis))
- **Creators:** nano (1–10k) respond to cold outreach at ~20–30%, micro at ~15–25%; they out-engage macro ~3x in community settings. Personalized gifting/seeding beats paid promo at this stage. People already tagging/mentioning you are pre-qualified advocates — activate them first. ([digitalapplied.com](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/influencer-marketing-2026-micro-nano-strategy), [influenceflow.io](https://influenceflow.io/resources/influencer-outreach-best-practices-a-complete-2026-guide/))
- **Testers:** founders source them ~evenly from existing waitlist (~29%), warm network (~26%), and online communities (~27%). 50 engaged testers beat 500 passive ones. Be a visible contributor in a community before recruiting from it. ([usersnap.com](https://usersnap.com/blog/free-beta-users-for-beta-testing/), [eleken.co](https://www.eleken.co/blog-posts/how-to-find-beta-testers))
- **Priority stack for emerging brands:** UGC → community → product seeding → customer conversations → micro-creator partnerships → founder-led storytelling → social proof → feedback loops.

## Weekly autonomous cycle (runs via the scheduled Routine; also on any session open)

1. **Dashboard:** wins / risks / opportunities / experiment queue → new `growth/2026-MM-DD_growth_week-NN_actions_v1.md`.
2. **Research sprint (sourced, current — never recycled advice):** one competitor/adjacent-account scan, one customer-language harvest (Reddit, LinkedIn, communities — exact quotes into `customer-language_v1.md`), one tactic check (what's working now).
3. **Tester engine:** advance the funnel in `2026-08-24_growth_tester-funnel_v1.md` — every week must add named candidate sources or outreach-ready drafts, not theory.
4. **Creator pipeline:** research real nano/micro candidates (name, platform, size, niche, why-them, contact path, collab idea, response likelihood) — only verified real accounts, never invented; if research access limits verification, list leads as "to verify" rather than asserting.
5. **Content package:** only what passes the validation gate, sized to her actual week (her floor rules), each piece tagged with the outcome it serves.
6. **Honest ledger:** what was NOT done and why — silent truncation is forbidden.
