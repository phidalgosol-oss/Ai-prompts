## The hard thing first

**Maca Riva is macro (1.1M), not nano/micro.** Cold DMs from an 11k account to a 1.1M creator convert badly. Two consequences:

1. **Don't cold-pitch her. Earn the room she's already in.** She guests on podcasts ([PUNCH Podcast](https://punchpodcast.buzzsprout.com/1241294/episodes/16134373-macarena-riva-storytelling-para-negocios-t5-e14)) and appears on LATAM business-leader platforms. The realistic path to that tier is *being a guest in the same ecosystem*, not a DM.
2. **The people who will actually reply are one or two tiers down** — nano (1–10k) reply to cold outreach at ~20–30%, micro (10–100k) at ~15–25% ([Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/influencer-marketing-2026-micro-nano-strategy)). That tier is where the connection strategy earns its keep.

**What "like Maca Riva" actually means for the archetype:** Spanish-speaking woman · operator/founder credibility (not just commentary) · makes intimidating business/tech topics feel human and funny · builds in public across IG + LinkedIn + podcast. **That is Priscilla's own archetype in a different vertical** — she is not a fan reaching up; she's a peer in an adjacent lane (AI + work + cross-border trade instead of corporate finance).

## Engagement ladder (how a stranger becomes a collaborator)

1. **Weeks 1–2 — be visible:** substantive comments on their posts (3–5 targets, 2x/week). Not "great post 🔥" — an actual thought only she could add.
2. **Week 3 — give first:** share their work with her own framing, tag them. No ask attached.
3. **Week 4 — the human ask:** DM referencing the specific exchange → collab idea sized to them (guest swap, joint live, her analysis on their audience's question).
4. **Ongoing:** long-term relationships beat one-off placements at this tier.
