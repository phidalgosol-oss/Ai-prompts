# Warm Tester Outreach — DxVida (v1, 2026-08-24)

**Offer status: APPROVED by Priscilla (2026-08-24).**
Testers get: free full access during beta · their real import/export case analyzed · direct line to the founder · named early-supporter status at launch (with permission). Cohort 1 cap: 20–30. Send to warm network first — ~26% of founders' testers come from warm network ([usersnap](https://usersnap.com/blog/free-beta-users-for-beta-testing/)).

## Rules for sending

- **One at a time, personalized.** Replace every `[...]` — a note that could have been sent to anyone converts like a note that was.
- **Max 10 per week.** This is a small pond; spray-and-pray burns it.
- **One follow-up only**, 5–7 days later, and only if they didn't answer.
- **No selling.** Nothing is for sale — you're asking for their brain, not their money. Say so.
- **Never invent numbers.** The credibility here comes from precision about *the ask* (20 minutes, one case, 20–30 people), not from startup stats.

> Priscilla
